Scientific argument

Main claim. Boundary realism is the controlling variable for the actin-ratchet F-V relationship, and a faithful Mem3DG↔ReaDDy coupler is necessary to recover the flexible-membrane regime that rigid models cannot produce.

Evidence for

  • [OBSERVED] Fixed and rigid rungs share one actin model; only the barrier changes — a clean controlled comparison, and both run.
  • [OBSERVED] In the fixed rung barrier_velocity holds at ~0 while contact_force accumulates.
  • [OBSERVED] In the flexible rung the vesicle inflates under actin pressure.
  • [INFERRED] Closed-form Peskin (1993) expectations bracket the two rigid rungs — falsifiable, but the comparison has not been run.

Evidence against / open

  • [OPEN] All acceptance tests are still stubs; no quantitative match has been demonstrated.
  • [OPEN] The concave→convex F-V transition (Inoue 2015) is asserted by construction, not measured — vesicle inflation alone is NOT evidence of ratchet behavior.
  • [OPEN] Newton's-3rd-law balance and mesh-quality preservation are inferred from design, not yet measured.

Caveats

  • The strongest supported conclusion today is WORKFLOW READINESS, not biological validation.
  • Coupler faithfulness (Newton's 3rd law, mesh quality) is a precondition; if it fails the F-V comparison is uninterpretable.
  • ReaDDy reaction balance under per-step boundary updates is assumed, not yet proven.
  • Current visuals emphasize that simulations ran; they do not yet reveal the mechanism (gap formation, force transfer, load redistribution).

Glossary

Brownian ratchet
Mechanism converting thermal fluctuations at a barrier into directed motion when polymerization rectifies the transient gap.
F-V curve
Force–velocity relationship of the advancing barrier; its shape (concave vs convex) is the diagnostic signature.
Peskin 1993
Closed-form theory for a Brownian ratchet against a rigid load; sets the rigid-rung expectations.
Inoue 2015
Flexible-membrane ratchet study showing a concave→convex F-V transition with polymerization rate — the headline benchmark.
Mem3DG
Mesh-based discrete-differential-geometry membrane simulator providing the deformable boundary.
ReaDDy
Particle-based reaction-diffusion simulator providing the actin filaments and tip dynamics.
BrownianRatchetCoupler
The composite Step that exchanges tip forces and boundary positions between ReaDDy and Mem3DG each tick.
